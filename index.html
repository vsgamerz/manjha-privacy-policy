Privacy Policy — Manjha: Kite Fight

Last updated: 27th July, 2026

This policy explains what Manjha (com.vsgamerz.manjha) collects and why. In short: the game itself stores your scores on your own device, and the only data leaving your phone is what our advertising provider collects and — if you post a score — the name you choose for the leaderboard.

What is stored on your device

Your high scores, chosen display name, difficulty preference, and sound and vibration settings are saved locally in the app. They are not sent anywhere unless described below. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

Manjha shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and interactions with ads. Google acts as an independent controller of that data.

  • Google's practices: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Where required by law, you will be shown a consent form before any personalised ads are loaded, and you may change that choice later.

Online leaderboard

If the global leaderboard is enabled, submitting a score stores the following in Google Firebase (Firestore):

DataWhy
The display name you choose To show who holds the score. Pick any name — it does not have to be your real one.
Your score and difficulty modeTo rank entries.
An anonymous account identifier A random id generated by Firebase Anonymous Authentication so your own entry can be updated when you beat it. It is not linked to a Google account, email address, or phone number.
A timestampTo order tied scores.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or files.

Children

Manjha is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted a display name you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app clears everything held on your device. To have a leaderboard entry removed, email the address below with the display name and approximate score; we will delete the matching entry.

A note on real kite flying

Manjha is a work of fiction. Glass-coated and metallic kite string causes serious injury to people, birds and animals every season and is banned in many places. Please fly with plain cotton thread, in open ground, away from roads and power lines.
