Privacy Policy — Manjha: Kite Fight

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what Manjha (com.vsgamerz.manjha) collects and why. In short: the game itself stores your scores, kite unlocks, and settings on your own device. Data that can leave your phone is what our advertising provider collects, anonymous gameplay events sent to Firebase Analytics when that service is configured, and — if you post a score — the name you choose for the leaderboard.

What is stored on your device

Your high scores, chosen display name, kite colour, cut progress, daily goal, difficulty preference, and sound and vibration settings are saved locally in the app. A reminder for the next evening, if you allow notifications, is scheduled on the device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes the local data.

Advertising

Manjha shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and interactions with ads. Google acts as an independent controller of that data.

  • Google's practices: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Where required by law, you will be shown a consent form before the advertising SDK starts, and you may change that choice later.

Analytics

When Firebase Analytics is configured for this app, the game sends anonymous event names (such as the app starting, a kite being cut, or a round ending) together with the round's score, cut count, length in seconds, and difficulty. Firebase also receives its usual app-instance identifier and device information so those events can be counted. Events are not tied to your leaderboard name. If analytics is not configured, nothing is sent.

Online leaderboard

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or files.

Children

Manjha is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted a display name you would like removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app clears everything held on your device. To have a leaderboard entry removed, email the address below with the display name and approximate score; we will delete the matching entry.

A note on real kite flying

Manjha is a work of fiction. Glass-coated and metallic kite string causes serious injury to people, birds and animals every season and is banned in many places. Please fly with plain cotton thread, in open ground, away from roads and power lines.
